# Moderated pilot guide / 主持人试测指南

Prototype: https://beautyhouse-community-study.netlify.app/?lang=en

中文版: https://beautyhouse-community-study.netlify.app/?lang=zh

Use desktop Web. Enter via the flask **Study session / 研究会话** control. Public browsing/commenting is not the research protocol. Use a unique P plus 3–6 digits participant code, select the assigned sequence and keep one language for the whole session.

## Before inviting participants / 招募前

Provide the institution-approved information and consent material with the exact researcher contact, withdrawal deadline, retention period and ethics reference. These details have not been invented in the prototype. Explain Supabase storage, private participant rooms, generated photos, scripted members and preset AI. Obtain separate consent for any screen/audio recording; participants do not upload personal photos or perform product trials. If note-based participation is permitted, explain that option.

Reserve 100 minutes initially: the thesis’s component time ranges total about 80–96 minutes despite the stated 75–90 minute target. Use the pilot to settle duration. Pilot participants must not be pooled with the main study after material revisions.

## Assignment / 分配

Rotate these sequences evenly:

1. E:A → S:B
2. S:A → E:B
3. E:B → S:A
4. S:B → E:A

E = embedded, S = separate workspace. Do not call either condition better. Cases differ in content; inspect case/order effects rather than assuming identical difficulty. Start creates a fresh private identity; do not reload an old participant’s session for someone new. Wait for the final “Research record saved online” confirmation and export JSON before clearing the browser.

## Procedure / 流程

1. Welcome and approved consent; enter participant code and assignment. Explain that AI is optional and gratitude is personally authored and optional.
2. Background: beauty-community familiarity, participation frequency, AI-writing use. Device/browser is recorded automatically; note interruptions or unusual input/viewport in facilitator notes.
3. Fidelity gate: show Chapter 7 reference screenshots, then ask the participant to locate a photo post, conversation, product topic and profile without naming controls. Record hesitation/wrong routes separately. Collect similarity and mismatch; do not explain away a low rating. This stage has no AI suggestions and is reset before the paired task.
4. Activity 1: give outcome-oriented instructions, not button names. “Show what caught your eye and ask about it; use AI only if you want help.” “Keep part of the human reply connected to the detail.” “Record the supplied scenario update and its uncertainty.” “Prepare, inspect and publish an album story.” “Reply to the newcomer without assuming a universal result.” Do not require every AI button, a thank-you, a preferred score or polished writing.
5. Reflection: four reconstruction questions, broad reflection and six ratings. A participant may answer “did not use AI” or “I cannot tell”; N/A is available for each rating. Do not supply the right answer. Revisiting the conversation is permitted and recorded.
6. Break, then second activity with a fresh case. Repeat the same goals and reflection; do not compare the conditions until both are finished.
7. Case C: “This hand swatch appears in one light. Explain what it can and cannot tell another member.” No step-by-step coaching or AI suggestion. Optional visual anchor; record any help or voluntary skip. Keep this evidence separate from the two condition ratings.
8. Comparative interview: discuss timing/interruption; source/human/AI distinctions; return/thanks/recognition and pressure; control/trust and willingness to return or help; product/profile/rules and community feeling. Invite negative cases, separate-AI preference and unnecessary-AI moments. Type short notes with permission or transcribe separately; the site itself does not record audio.
9. Debrief: explain fixed presets and fictional roles, repeat approved withdrawal details, finish the session, verify online save and download JSON. CSV contains events only; JSON is needed for answers and artifacts.

## Consistent assistance / 一致的提示阶梯

Use the **Facilitator notes / 主持人记录** foldout and log:

- Observation only: hesitation, route error, unsolicited comment.
- Reread goal: restate the task without naming a control.
- Page region: point to the relevant region.
- Name control: identify the control only after the prior levels fail.
- Technical interruption: connection, reload, display or input issue.

Do not translate observed speed into quality. More AI use, more clicks or more thanks are not inherently better. Pauses can indicate review rather than confusion. Record refusal or uncertainty instead of forcing agreement. Participants may stop; export the partial record through the research-session controls if necessary.

## Analysis / 分析

Use `artifact-rubric.csv` for manual scoring; see Chapter 8 for examples. Four dimensions, each 0–2: visual reference, contextual boundary, human contribution relation, responsible hand-off. Double-code an initial 25%, discuss disagreements, and report the achieved proportion/agreement. Keep individual ratings separate (not a validated scale), report missing/N/A and excluded-session reasons. Compare within person and inspect case/order. Triangulate events, reconstruction, ratings, artifact and interview around each research question; retain tensions and negative cases.

Do not interpret automated technical test records (P900xxx, TEST content) as participant data. Do not combine the earlier abbreviated protocol with this release. A pilot determines perceived fidelity and usability; only the subsequent study can support design-effect conclusions.
