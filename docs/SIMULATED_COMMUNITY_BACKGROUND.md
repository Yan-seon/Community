# Fictional community background imported on 15 October 2026

This release adds 20 authored fictional characters, 35 conversations, 136 public contributions and 18 scripted appreciations. It is a dataset for exploring the final community state. It is not a seven-day observation or a 20-person participant study. The 136 contributions comprise 128 ordinary comments/returns, three image-anchored questions, three reviewed experience records and two source-linked experience replies.

The characters have different continuing interests and writing habits. Five primarily ask questions, five primarily share visual or everyday experiences, four tend to help, four maintain social conversations, and two compare purchases or preferences. These are overlapping design roles, informed by a small purposive review of Sephora BIC, the fan-run r/glossier community and Samsung Members. The numbers are simulation parameters, not measured population percentages. The review does not estimate lurkers, user demographics or AI attitudes from public posts. Sephora remains the case anchor; Samsung contributes interaction-pattern comparisons, not technology-support subject matter.

The local research audit records the sampled contexts, observation limits, source links and translation from evidence to scenario choices. Discussion text is newly authored. Original members are not impersonated and their full posts are not republished. Thirty-two selected Wikimedia Commons reference photographs retain their authors, source and reuse licence. Images are not presented as the fictional characters' own photographs or as proof of product effects. Complete image attribution is available at `/community-media/credits.json` and through the conversation disclosure.

All imported rows contain `synthetic_seed: true` and a `simulation` object identifying the batch, character, sources and fictional narrative time. `created_at` remains the actual database insertion time. The seven-day scenario history spans 8–14 October 2026; the import date is separate. Imported content does not create `study_sessions` or ratings. A single dedicated import identity manages all 20 fictional characters; this is not 20 independent auth users.

The batch uses stable UUIDs. Re-running its import checks existing IDs and inserts only missing records, without deleting or overwriting existing comments. Quote dependencies are inserted before replies. A private local backup of prior shared rows and import credentials is kept outside this repository. No administrative Supabase key is required or published.

Images are deployed once as Netlify static files, rather than duplicated into comment JSON or hotlinked from community sites. Their total payload is 2,111,368 bytes. Database batch size and successful persisted counts are recorded in the local import report; official subscription limits must not be confused with account-specific unused capacity.

The original case scenes and real visitor commenting remain available. Normal visits reveal the simulated-member label. Clicking a member opens their fictional profile and authored conversations. References, quotations and experience versions remain inspectable. No daily scheduler, task gates or required participation quota has been added.
