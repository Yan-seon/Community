# Embedded community practices · 15 October 2026

Version 4.5.0 retains the desktop community shell. It extends the authored background with 66 contributions, including 28 explicit instances of the five intervention moments: eight image questions, eight sentence follow-ups, four discussion bridges, four reviewed records and four qualified experience replies. The remaining contributions include human explanations, updates, disagreement, ordinary ritual and non-adoption. They illustrate a design proposition; they are not recruited participants or observed AI effects.

The design returns to the thesis's practice account: expression and identity, social connection, documenting and mentoring, and situated brand use. AI carries an object and its context across these practices, while people choose their words, help one another, return, disagree and decide what to publish. It does not award honour, imitate a helper, generate recognition or automate a consensus.

## Interaction changes

- Click a detail on the post image to begin a question; previously published image questions remain accessible through pins.
- Pin geometry uses the photograph's natural aspect ratio in the post, composer, public thumbnail and enlarged reference.
- Select a member sentence to retain their exact wording, attribution and original-comment locator in a reply.
- Local question help offers explicit intentions: explain a detail, ask about conditions, or invite a different experience. It does not interpret the photograph.
- Related-discussion presets use declared topic vocabulary, groups and shared words. Incidental words such as “reference/photo” do not dominate matching. Previews, selection and return remain voluntary in embedded and separate arrangements.
- “AI helped…” details expose the public reference, role, member decision and source links. Before/after examples are supplied only for authored background. A visitor's original draft is not exposed through this component.
- Ordinary browsing no longer repeats a synthetic-member badge on every contribution. Community information centrally explains fictional seed content and preset AI. Licensing remains with each reference photograph; backend provenance and study exclusions remain intact.

`data/community-practice-episodes.json` is the versioned authored stimulus package. Dedicated operator identity and actual server creation times are preserved. No existing visitor contribution, participant session, feedback or research outcome is generated or overwritten by the import.

The five mechanisms do not form a mandatory journey. Non-adoption, disagreement, quiet reading, unsuccessful attempts and aesthetic appreciation are valid community activity. An activity count or illustrative episode cannot demonstrate increased reciprocity, belonging or long-term loyalty.
